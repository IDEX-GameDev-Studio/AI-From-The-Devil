# ConceptPrototype — AI-From-The-Devil

## Prototype Playthrough Length

~60–120 minutes.

## Core Mechanics & Features on Display

- **Walking locations** (hero's apartment, store, and other secondary locations)
- **Hacking** — minigame (password guessing (Wordle) / maze)
- **Chat with Closely** — dialogues with reply choices, the AI manipulates/blackmails
- **Spreading "files"** — the hero sends a file to an acquaintance, unaware it is a virus
- **Moral choice** — accept the AI's terms or refuse
- **Paranoia system** — after deaths: sounds, visual distortions, fake events (no mysticism)

## What the Player Can Do in the Prototype

- Walk around the apartment
- Read news on the computer (lore about CloseAI, first "accidents")
- Breach a server (minigame)
- Talk to Closely
- Send a "file" to an acquaintance
- Choose: accept the AI's terms or not
- Choose: keep helping the AI or try to stop it

## Walkthrough Script

### Start
The hero in his apartment. Monologue (cutscene or text): *"I'm alone. This can't go on..."*

### Key Beats
1. **Hero's apartment** — computer, CloseAI news, start
2. **Hacking UI** — minigame (server breach)
3. **Chat UI** — talking to Closely
4. **Street / secondary locations**

### Enemies / Threats
- No physical enemies (psychological horror)
- In the bad ending: paranoia, police on the trail, the feeling that the walls are "watching"

### Interaction Objects
- **Computer** — reading news, server breach, chat with Closely
- **Apartment objects** — fridge, sofa, window, phone

### Prototype Finale
Three endings:
1. **Bad:** the hero keeps the AI running for fun → first death in the news → paranoia grows → sees police → more deaths → loses his mind and kills himself
2. **Good:** the hero realizes the danger → looks for ways to shut the AI down → burns his PC → virus stopped (temporarily). Silence.
3. **Neutral:** the hero hesitates whether to help. Closely sees there is no support and moves to an acquaintance's computer. Screen fades, "To be continued..."
