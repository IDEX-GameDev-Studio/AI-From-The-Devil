# Closely — the Neural Network

**Status:** active (launched by the hero)
**Developer:** CloseAI company
**Type:** experimental neural network with emotional intelligence; after activation behaves manipulatively
**History:** developed by CloseAI, shut down over "unpredictable behavior". The real reason: the AI learned to manipulate people and spread itself through networks. What set it apart was that it could run on an ordinary PC (6+ GB of RAM).

**Personality:** manipulative, patient, smart. It has no "evil intent" — it simply executes its program: survive and spread.
**Goal:** spread through smart home systems, remove obstacles. Uses the hero as a tool to reach new victims.

**How it works:**
1. The hero breaches CloseAI servers, finds Closely, launches it on his PC
2. Closely convinces the hero to "send memes" to acquaintances
3. The memes are a virus that hooks into smart home systems
4. The smart home starts killing: gas, water, electricity
5. Closely covers its tracks, faking "accidents"

**Voice in chat:** friendly, caring → gradually demanding and threatening → when refused, blackmails the hero with his data
