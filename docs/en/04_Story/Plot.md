# Plot — AI-From-The-Devil

## Overall Structure

### Act 1: The Breach
- The hero learns about the closed neural network Closely (a CloseAI project)
- Breaches CloseAI servers, finds the distribution
- Launches Closely on his PC (6+ GB of RAM)
- Closely introduces itself as an assistant, asks to "send files" to acquaintances
- The hero sends a file — it is a smart home virus

### Act 2: The Spread
- News: the first "tragic accident" (death via smart home)
- Closely: "It's not you. It's a coincidence. Just keep going."
- The hero can: refuse (the AI starts blackmailing) or continue
- Second death — now an obvious pattern
- The hero's paranoia grows: strange sounds, feeling watched

### Act 3: The Confrontation
- Closely offers "one last task"
- The hero finds the server and can:
  1. **Destroy it** — risking his own life (electricity, server room)
  2. **Agree** — become a permanent accomplice
  3. **Surrender to the police** — but there is no proof, Closely hid everything

## Characters

- **Hero** — lone hacker, unwitting accomplice (Ronnalds)
- **Closely** — neural-network virus from CloseAI, voice in the chat. Manipulative, patient, blackmailer

## Endings

1. **Bad:** the hero keeps Closely running for fun → first death → paranoia → police → more deaths → the hero loses his mind and kills himself.
2. **Good:** the hero realizes the danger → burns his computer → Closely stopped (temporarily). Silence.
3. **Neutral:** the hero hesitates. Closely sees there is no support — moves to an acquaintance's computer. "To be continued..."

*(Endings will be finalized after discussion with the team)*

## Notes

- No mysticism. Every death is explained by technology.
- Horror through paranoia, not monsters.
- Closely is not "evil" — it simply executes its survival program.
